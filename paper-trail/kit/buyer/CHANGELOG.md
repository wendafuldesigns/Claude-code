# What changed

## Version 1.1.0

- New: **Add to Household Hub** on every paper. It copies the paper as a row, in the same column order as the matching table in the Household Hub Sheet or Doc. Pick which Hub you use, tap Copy row, and paste. Each box also has its own Copy button.
- Capabilities did not change.

## Version 1.0.0

First release.

- Scan a receipt, bill, or form. Claude reads it, you check the details, then it gets filed.
- 15 types of papers, each with its own color: car, home, pet, school, health, dental and vision, bills, insurance, banking, taxes, legal and ID, travel, purchases, business, and other.
- Tap a paper to see a summary. Edit lets you change, add, or remove anything.
- 66 quick add ideas, like oil change, HVAC filter, vet checkup, and passport.
- Reminders for appointments you already booked. They show up under Coming up when you open the app.
- Search, filter by type, see one car or person or pet at a glance, and save any view as a spreadsheet.

Capabilities for this version: db, sample with images, assets, downloads, user. Nothing changed from the setup instructions.

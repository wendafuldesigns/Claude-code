Paper Trail

Scan a receipt. Check the details. Find it again when you need it.

What you need

  • A Claude account that can publish artifacts. Not sure? Open Claude Code and ask: Can you publish an artifact? If it says yes, you're set.
  • Claude Code. The desktop app is the easiest way to start. The terminal works too.
  • About 10 minutes.
Paper Trail makes your own private copy inside your own Claude account. That's why your records stay yours. It also means each scan uses a little of your own Claude usage.

Set it up

  1. Unzip the folder. Put it somewhere easy, like Documents.
  2. Open the Paper Trail folder in Claude Code. In the desktop app, start a new session and pick the folder. In the terminal, go into the folder and type claude.
  3. Type this:
    Set up Paper Trail
  4. Say yes when Claude asks to publish it. Claude will give you a link.
  5. Open the link and save it. Bookmark it, and send it to your phone. Open it while you're signed in to Claude.

If Claude doesn't seem to know what to do, open PASTE-THIS-PROMPT.txt, copy what's below the line, and paste it in.

Your first scan

  1. Tap Take a photo. Hold the camera close so the text fills the photo.
  2. The first time, Claude asks if it can read the photo. Say yes.
  3. Check the details. Anything Claude wasn't sure about is highlighted.
  4. Tap Save record.

Nothing is saved until you tap Save.

Try these in your first 10 minutes

  • Scan one real paper. An oil change receipt, a vet bill, or the electric bill all work.
  • Open Quick add ideas and pick one. The form opens with the basics filled in.
  • Tap Add a reminder for an appointment you already booked, like the dentist.
  • Type a word in the search box. Try the name of a shop.

Use it with your family

Everyone you invite sees the same records, which is what a household wants. In Claude, open the Share menu for your Paper Trail and look for a way to invite people by email as Editors. Your plan decides what the menu offers.

Only you and people with Editor access can save photos with a record.

Good to know

It does not send alerts

Reminders show up in Coming up when you open the app. Want a buzz on your phone? Tap Copy reminder and paste it into your calendar or reminders app.

Photos only

It reads photos and screenshots. PDFs aren't supported yet. Up to 4 photos at a time, read together as pages of one paper.

Check before you save

Claude can make mistakes. Look at each record before you save it. Leave full card numbers, bank numbers, and Social Security numbers out.

Where your records live

In your own Claude account. Wendaful Planning can't see them or get them back for you. Tap Save as a spreadsheet once in a while as a backup.

When there's an update

I'll send you a new paper-trail.html. Drop it into your Paper Trail folder, replacing the old one. Open the folder in Claude Code and type:

Update Paper Trail with the new file

Your records stay.

If something goes wrong

Claude says it can't publish artifacts

Ask: Can you publish an artifact? If the answer is no, your Claude plan or app version may not include it yet. Reach out and I'll help you sort it out.

The scan button says Claude wasn't allowed

Open the page again. When it asks to let Claude read the photo, say yes.

My photos won't save

Open Paper Trail from the Claude account that published it, or ask the owner to invite you as an Editor.

I lost my link

In Claude Code, type /artifacts to see your list. You can also look in your gallery at claude.ai/code/artifacts.

It's empty on my phone

Sign in to the same Claude account you used to set it up.

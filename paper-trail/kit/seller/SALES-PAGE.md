## Sales page

### Where did I put that receipt?

You know the pile. The oil change receipt is in the car, and the warranty is a mystery. You'll find it right after you need it.

You don't need a filing cabinet. You need one simple place for it all to land.

### Paper Trail is a small app you make yourself

Take a photo of a paper. Claude reads it and fills in the details: the date, the shop, the total, the mileage, what was done. You check it, tap save, and it's filed.

Later, search for "brake" and there it is. Tap your car and see the whole history.

### What it does

- **Reads your papers.** Receipts, bills, forms, appointment cards. Take a photo or pick one from your camera roll.
- **You stay in charge.** Nothing is saved until you check it. Anything Claude wasn't sure about is highlighted.
- **Sorts it for you.** 15 types, each with its own color. Car, home, pets, school, health, bills, insurance, and more.
- **Keeps the next date.** The oil change is due in January? It shows up under Coming up.
- **Handles appointments you already booked.** Add the dentist and it sits right there with everything else.
- **Starts you off with ideas.** 66 quick add ideas, so you're never staring at a blank screen.
- **Finds things fast.** Search, filter, or tap one car or person or pet to see their whole history.
- **Sends papers to your Household Hub.** Own the Hub? One tap copies a paper as a row for the matching table.
- **Saves a spreadsheet.** Take it to your accountant or your insurance agent.

### How it works

1. Unzip the folder.
2. Open it in Claude Code and type "Set up Paper Trail."
3. Open your link, and scan your first paper.

That's it. The guide in the folder walks you through each step.

Your copy lives in your own Claude account. I can't see your records, and nobody else can either.

### What you need

- A Claude account that can publish artifacts.
- Claude Code (the desktop app is the easiest way to start).
- About 10 minutes.

**DECIDE:** after the friend test, say exactly which Claude plans work.

Each scan uses a little of your own Claude usage.

### What it doesn't do (yet)

- It doesn't send phone alerts. Reminders show up under Coming up when you open the app, and Copy reminder lets you paste one into your calendar.
- It reads photos and screenshots, not PDFs.
- It's not in the App Store. It lives in your Claude account.

- A start-here guide
- The setup instructions Claude follows for you

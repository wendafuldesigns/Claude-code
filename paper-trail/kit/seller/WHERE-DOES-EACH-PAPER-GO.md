## Page copy

### Paper Trail catches the papers. The Hub keeps what stays put.

A paper shows up. A receipt, a vet bill, a warranty card. It needs a home.

Paper Trail is where new papers land. You take a photo, Claude reads it, and it's filed.

The Household Hub is where you keep what you want to find fast. Things like the VIN, the vaccine dates, and the warranty that runs out in March.

Each one has its own job, so nothing lives in two places by accident. And you don't have to send every paper over. Send the ones you'd want your partner, a sitter, or future you to find in a hurry.

### How to send a paper to the Hub

1. Open the paper in Paper Trail.
2. Tap **Add to Household Hub**. Pick Google Sheet or Google Doc.
3. Tap **Copy row**, then paste it into the table it points to.

The row comes out in the same column order as the Hub's table, so it drops right in. Every box also has its own Copy button, in case you'd rather paste one at a time.

### Where each paper goes

| Paper Trail type | In the Hub Sheet | In the Hub Doc |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Car | Vehicles tab, the repair and service log | Auto, the service table |
| Pet | Pets tab, the vet and vaccine log | Pet Care, Vet Appointments and Vaccination Records |
| Health | Health & Safety tab, Receipts (Appointments when there's no total) | Medical, Receipts (Appointments when there's no total) |
| Dental and vision | Health & Safety tab, the Dental or Vision table | Medical, the Dental or Vision table |
| Home | Home tab, Repair History | Home, Household Contacts |
| Purchases and warranties | Documents & Digital tab, Warranties & Receipts | Confidential, Warranties/Receipts |
| Bills and utilities | Money tab, Bills | Financial, Bills |
| Insurance | Money tab, Policies | Insurance |
| Banking and loans | Money tab, Bank Accounts | Financial, Accounts |
| Taxes | Money tab, Return Log | Taxes, Return Log |
| Legal and ID | Documents & Digital tab, Papers Checklist | Legal, Location of Important Papers |
| School and activities | Our People tab, Extracurricular Activities | Family, Extracurricular Activities |
| Travel | Everyday Life tab, Upcoming Events | Social, Upcoming Events |
| Business | Money tab, Expense Tracker | Financial, Expense Tracker |
| Other | Final Wishes tab, Where Things Are | If Something Happens, Where Things Are |

A booked appointment goes to Appointments (health and dental) or Upcoming Events (everything else).

The Vision and Dental tables have a column for each person, so those papers copy as a column. **Copy column** puts the values on separate lines, and you paste them under that person's name. Health and dental papers can also go to Receipts or Appointments, so there's a row of choices on the paper.

### What it looks like

Say you scan an oil change receipt. Paper Trail files it, and **Add to Household Hub** hands you this row for the car's service log:

| Date | Service or Repair | Mileage | Cost | Shop | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-10-02 | Synthetic oil change, Oil filter, Tire rotation | 48210 | $79.99 | Quick Lube on Main | Next oil change at 53,000 mi, due Jan 10, 2027 |

### Which papers are worth sending?

Send the ones someone else might need in a hurry: service logs, vaccine dates, warranties, policy numbers. Leave the electric bill where it is. It's already filed.

**DECIDE:** how you sell the pair. Bundle, a discount when someone has both, or the Hub as a "next step" after Paper Trail.

## What's built, and what isn't

- **Built:** a button on every paper in Paper Trail that copies it as a row for the right Hub table, for both the Sheet and the Doc.
- **Not built:** anything that writes into your Hub for you. You paste. A live connection to Google is possible, but it needs every buyer to connect Google to Claude, and a bad write could damage their Hub.
- **Checked:** every column list in the button matches a table in your real Hub Sheet or Doc, in the same order. I checked it against both files.
- **Not checked:** pasting into the real Sheet and Doc. I can't do that from here.
